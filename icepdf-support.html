icepdf support

Commercial support for icepdf.

icepdf is an open-source (Apache 2.0) Java library for rendering, viewing, annotating, signing and redacting PDF. Corless Services, the company behind Excisa™, offers paid support for it, from the developer who maintains it.

Support

One yearly subscription per organisation, priced by size. It covers every product and team in the organisation, and includes the icepdf maintenance fee where that applies.

Answers from the maintainer

A private e-mail line for questions about rendering, forms, signatures, redaction, integration and upgrading from older versions.

Your issues first

Problems you report are triaged ahead of the public queue. Fixes go into the open-source project, so they stay in every later release.

Invoiced

A yearly invoice your finance team can pay. No licence keys, and icepdf behaves the same whether you subscribe or not.

Prices in US dollars, indicative during early access. Support is best effort with no guaranteed response times.

Priority fixes

A document that renders wrong, a form that won't fill, a signature that won't validate: send it, and you get a quote to fix it, scheduled ahead of other work. The fix goes into the open-source release. Your document stays private and is never added to the public test set without your permission.

Get in touch

Write to support@excisa.com with your icepdf version and what you use it for. icepdf support customers get a discount on Excisa.

icepdf stays free and open source; the issue tracker and discussions on GitHub are open to everyone. Corless Services is not affiliated with ICEsoft Technologies.
